FNAF
Morphs

Walk the pizzeria as an animatronic. Your head, hands and steps move the suit, so you can look down and see your own arms.

Menu
Tap the ≡ (3 lines) button floating over your left hand, or press Y, to open the morph menu. Point and pull the trigger.
Move
Left stick walks. Right stick snap-turns.
Springlocks
Wear Spring Bonnie or Springtrap and the springlocks give way after 1:30. The suit pops, you laugh, and then life goes on. A mirror is in the menu so you can watch.
