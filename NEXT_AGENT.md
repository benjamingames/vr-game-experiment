## The goal, in the owner's words: extreme, lifelike realism

The reference is a modern architectural-visualisation interior render. The *style and realism*
matter, not the specific objects. Judge every screenshot against a photo, not against "good for
WebGL". The owner has repeatedly rejected results that were merely nice. Specifically:

1. **Nothing flat or generic.** Walls, floors and ceilings need relief, varied sheen, wear and
   reflections. A tiled pattern on a flat plane is a failure.
2. **Objects must have real shape.** No furniture made of plain boxes. Real chairs, desks, lamps
   and sofas have curves, contours, soft edges, seams, indents, stitching, feet, fixings and
   several different materials each. Model those.
3. **Light must be soft and believable.** Indirect light, ambient occlusion, area lamps, warm
   concealed LEDs, sun patches. Use the GPU light bake (`bake.ts`) for anything static.
4. **Fine detail everywhere**: high-resolution generated textures, micro-bevels, material
   variation. If a surface looks computer-made up close, improve it.

## State

- Main room (`room.ts` + `decor.ts`): restyled, NOT baked (painted corner shadows), walls still
  flat planes. Toy stations are now modelled (`stations.ts`): hoop frame/rim mount/printed board,
  turned pedestals, xylophone on a real table, arcade whack-a-mole cabinet, high striker with
  printed scale and bulbs, leather punch bag, lane markings, opal-glass wall sconces.
- Plants everywhere are grown weeping figs (`plants.ts`). Oak grain was retuned (long fibres).
- The Gallery (`showcase.ts`, start with `?dbg=gallery`): baked lighting. Desk top is stained ash,
  the monitor shows a desktop image, the lamp shade glows with a falloff.
- Untested: walking through the doors between rooms, anything in a headset.

- Monke Map (`arena.ts`): crowns are instanced leaf sprays over a small shaded core, grass is
  dense arching tufts, ground has straw/litter and a colour drift. High tier draws roughly 2-3
  million triangles there; frame rate was NOT measured. Low tier keeps big cores, few sprays.
- RC car has a moulded one-piece shell (`carShell`); the rug has a bound edge.
- Lario, the stretchy head (`face.ts`): an original character (teal beanie, auburn hair, hazel
  eyes, roll collar). The face is one sculpted skin mesh; `LarioSkin` drags the skin around seven
  invisible grab handles (nose, cheeks, lips, lids, chin). Beanie and ears are separate pieces on
  elastic bridges. `?dbg=pull` shows a stretched pose. Real grabbing was NOT tested: only the
  debug pose. Mouth does not open; no lower lashes; ears are still simple.
